Nintendo Switch

Controlled through a separate phone app, which is the easiest console setup of the three.

DeviceAbout 10 minutesSource confidence: highChecked 20 August 2026

What to watch for

  • Voice chat in some games happens through a phone app, outside the console’s controls
  • eShop spending
  • Sharing screenshots and videos to social media

Before you start

  • Download the Nintendo Switch Parental Controls app on your phone — it’s separate from the main Nintendo app.
  • You’ll need the console in front of you once, to link it with a registration code.
  1. 1

    Link the app to the console

    Once linked, you manage everything from your phone without touching the console again.

    Nintendo Switch Parental Controls app

    1. Install the Nintendo Switch Parental Controls app and sign in with your Nintendo Account.
    2. The app gives you a registration code.
    3. On the console, go to System Settings, then Parental Controls, and enter the code.
    4. Set a PIN that your child doesn’t know.
  2. 2

    Set the restriction level

    The quickest way to match content to their age. You can fine-tune afterwards.

    App → Console settings → Restriction Level

    1. Choose the age band that describes your child.
    2. Or choose Custom to set software rating, posting to social media, and communication individually.
    Recommended by age
    7–11Child level
    11–12Pre-Teen level
    13–15Teen level
    16–17Custom, agreed together
  3. 3

    Restrict communication and social posting

    Stops screenshots being posted publicly and limits in-game communication with strangers.

    App → Console settings → Custom settings

    1. Turn off Posting to Social Media.
    2. Restrict Communicating with Others.
    3. Note that some games route voice chat through the Nintendo Switch Online phone app, which sits outside these controls — check that separately.
  4. 4

    Set a play-time limit and bedtime alarm

    The console can suspend the game at the limit rather than just showing a message, which removes the argument.

    App → Play-Time Limit

    1. Set a daily play-time limit, with different values for weekdays and weekends if you like.
    2. Set a bedtime alarm.
    3. Turn on Suspend Software if you want the limit to be firm rather than advisory.
  5. 5

    Block eShop spending

    Set up in a different place from everything else, which is why it gets missed.

    accounts.nintendo.com → Family Group

    1. Sign in at accounts.nintendo.com and open your Family Group.
    2. Select your child and turn on the eShop purchase restriction.
    3. Remove any saved card.

Safety check

  • Parental Controls app linked to the console
  • PIN set and secret
  • Restriction level set for their age
  • Social posting off
  • Play-time limit and bedtime alarm set
  • eShop purchases blocked

Worth talking about

  • Ask whether any of their games use the phone app for voice chat, since that’s outside these controls.
  • Agree the play-time limit together so the alarm isn’t a surprise.
  • Play something together. The Switch is good for that.
